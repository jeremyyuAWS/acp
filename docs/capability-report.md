# ACP — what a scan can prove about the Core 17

Generated by `scripts/gen_capability_report.py` from engine commit `6e5406b5`. Every column is a property of the engine, not of any document — this is reproducible from a checkout with no corpus and no network.

## The headline

- **17 criteria**, **62 (criterion, format) pairs** in `acp-core-17`.
- **17 of 62 pairs can certify a PASS.**
- **45 of 62 cannot** — a clean scan on those is *not* a conformance claim.

That second number is the one to read carefully, and it is not a backlog. A pair in `REVIEW_FORMATS` resolves to `REVIEW` when its detector fires and `NOT_EVALUATED` when it does not; it never resolves to `PASS`. So on those pairs a clean result means *nothing was found by a check that could not conclude either way* — which is a weaker statement than "this document conforms", and is the single most misreadable thing in the product.

`NOT_EVALUATED` is deliberately not called *not applicable*: the honest claim is that nobody looked, not that the criterion does not apply.

## Lanes

- **Assessment** — can the engine reach a verdict deterministically (🟢), only gather evidence for a human (🟡), or not at all (🔴)?
- **Remediation** — `AUTO` writes the fix back; `ASSISTED` drafts one for approval; `HUMAN` means a person does the work.

- **Assessment**: `review` 44, `auto` 17, `human` 1

## What this report does not tell you

- **Whether the detectors are correct.** Every column here is what the engine *claims*; none of it is measured against a document. A pair can sit in `AUTO` and still be wrong.
- **Anything about PDF, measured locally.** The PDF analyser is loaded at runtime from `ACP_PDF_ENGINE` and is not vendored into this repo, so PDF pairs are unrunnable on a fresh checkout — including in CI, where the PDF-dependent suites skip. The lanes below are still accurate; only observation is missing.
- **Whether the AI lane produces usable output.** `ASSISTED` says a draft is offered, not that it is good. That is a question for `scripts/bench_models.py --ladder`, which compares models on the criteria that actually call one.
